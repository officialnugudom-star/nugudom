How to log Spotify listeners
  1. Click the ↗ next to a nugu's name — opens their Spotify artist page in a new tab.
  2. Find "Monthly Listeners" on that page (it sits right under the artist photo).
  3. Copy the number, come back here, paste it into the input box for that nugu. Press Enter to jump to the next row.
  4. When you're done, hit Save all at the bottom — it writes everything in one go.
YouTube subs are auto-pulled — just hit the red button below. No copying needed.

What do we do?

CRAXY's song "NUGUDOM" criticizes those who purposefully ignore and mock nugus, (nugus = smaller K-pop artists). Expanding on this idea, the K-pop industry is dominated by major companies, meaning smaller groups are overshadowed and never receive the recognition they deserve.

That is why we created NUGUDOM, a website meant to spread CRAXY's message and help smaller artists get the dedicated fans they rightfully deserve.
